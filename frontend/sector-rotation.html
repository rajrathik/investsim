◆ Sector Rotation

What does this tell me? This chart ranks every sector from #1 (best) to #12 (worst) each year. Notice how the top performer changes constantly — last year's winner is often next year's loser. This is called sector rotation. What should I do with this? ✓ Don't chase last year's winner. The data shows that leadership rotates. Piling into a sector just because it was #1 last year often leads to disappointment. ✓ Diversify across sectors. Since no one can predict which sector will lead next year, owning a broad mix (or VTI, the total market) smooths out the ups and downs. ✓ Look for consistency, not just #1 finishes. A sector that regularly finishes in the top half may be a steadier performer than one that swings between #1 and #12. Remember: If picking the winning sector each year were easy, everyone would be rich. History shows that broad diversification beats guessing.
