Leadership Count

How many times each sector finished #1, top 3, or bottom 3 over the selected period.

How to read this

Leadership rotates. A sector that finished #1 in one year has not consistently remained a leader the following year.

A broad mix tracks the average. Holding many sectors, or a total-market fund such as VTI, produces returns closer to the middle of this table rather than its extremes.

Consistency is visible separately from #1 finishes. Some sectors finish in the top half regularly; others alternate between the top and bottom of the ranking.

Worth noting: Market leadership changes frequently. Historical winners have not consistently remained leaders the next year.
