N-year return = geometric mean (CAGR) of that year's return and the next (N−1) years: [(1+rY) × (1+rY+1) × ... × (1+rY+N-1)]1/N − 1. Sorted newest year first. Returns are nominal total returns (dividends reinvested). Data: Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern School of Business — "Historical Returns on Stocks, Bonds and Bills," updated January 2026. This is the S&P 500 index itself, not the SPY ETF (which only exists since 1993 and differs slightly due to fees/tracking).
