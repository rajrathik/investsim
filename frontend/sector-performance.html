About the data

How returns are calculated

Total return = (price change + dividends) ÷ year-begin price. Dividends are counted as cash received and are not reinvested back into the ETF.

In-progress years

Cells with a dashed outline and an asterisk are the current, incomplete calendar year. They are measured against the latest month of data loaded so far rather than a full twelve months, and update automatically as more months are synced.

Column definitions

  • Price CAGR — compound annual growth rate from price appreciation only
  • Total CAGR — compound annual growth rate including dividends
  • Best / worst year — highest and lowest single-year total return
  • Positive years — years with a positive total return, against total years
  • Total dividends — sum of all cash distributions paid over the period
  • Avg dividend/yr — average annual cash dividend payout
