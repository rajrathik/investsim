Learn from market history

Investing becomes easier to understand when you can see how markets have behaved across many different environments. Strong years, difficult years, long recoveries and sudden changes are all part of the same history. Looking at that history does not tell us what will happen next, but it can give us useful perspective on what has happened before.

One of the most important things history shows is that return and risk come together. Investments that produced strong long-term growth also experienced periods of decline and uncertainty along the way. The size of a decline matters, but so does how long it lasted, how long recovery took, and what happened over the years that followed.

Time can also change the way market movements look. A difficult month may feel significant when it happens, while a much longer period can tell a different story. The same is true across sectors and investment styles — leadership changes, investments do not always move together, and the experience of holding them can be very different even when long-term returns appear similar.

That is the purpose of InvestSim: not to provide an answer about what the market will do next, but to make the history easier to explore. The more clearly we understand what investors have experienced in the past, the better context we have for thinking about risk, diversification, time and our own investment decisions.

History does not provide certainty. It provides perspective.
