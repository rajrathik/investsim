What should I do with this?

Higher correlation means two investments have historically moved more similarly. Lower correlation may provide greater diversification.

To build a smoother ride, pair sectors with low correlation (below 0.5). When one dips, the other may hold steady.

Example: Technology (XLK) and Utilities (XLU) often have low correlation — owning both spreads your risk better than owning two tech-like sectors.

Remember: True diversification isn't just owning many sectors — it's owning sectors that don't all move together.
