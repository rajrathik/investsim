How It Works

↑ Back to top
What It Does
  • ›Invests a monthly amount (optionally growing each year) across your chosen asset mix
  • ›Uses actual historical prices from Yahoo Finance — not projections or averages
  • ›Buys whole shares only at the monthly high price (conservative, worst-case entry)
  • ›Unspent cash accumulates per-ticker until enough to buy the next share
  • ›Dividends are tracked as cash and earn money market interest at the FRED federal funds rate
Steps to Run
  1. 1.Set monthly amount, optional annual deposit growth, and investment period
  2. 2.Pick tickers from the dropdown — search by symbol or name
  3. 3.Set allocation % using sliders — total must equal 100%
  4. 4.Click ▶ Run Simulation
What You'll See
  • ›6 summary tiles — Total Invested, Equity Value, Dividends, Cash Accrual, Portfolio Balance, MMF Benchmark
  • ›Interactive charts — hover for month-by-month growth and dividend details
  • ›Monthly table — click any highlighted cell for per-ticker detail
  • ›Tax impact — adjust rate to see yearly liability on dividends and interest
  • ›Annual returns — pre-tax performance per calendar year with clickable detail modals
Share Accumulation — Example
Setup: $1,000/month — 60% Holding A ($250/share), 40% Holding B ($800/share)

Month 1: $600 → A bucket → buy 2 shares ($500), carry $100  |  $400 → B bucket, not enough yet
Month 2: $700 for A → buy 2 shares, carry $200  |  $800 for B → buy 1 share, carry $0

Unavailable ticker: its budget shifts to the others that month, resumes next month.
