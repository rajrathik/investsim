Welcome to the Asset Allocation Simulator

A quick guide before you start

📈

What This Tool Does

  • Simulates investing a fixed monthly amount across ETFs, stocks, and funds
  • Uses historical prices from Yahoo Finance — not projections
  • Buys at the monthly high price (worst-case entry point)
  • Tracks dividends as cash — not reinvested into equities
🎯

How to Use It

  1. Set your monthly amount and investment period (1–20 years)
  2. Pick tickers from the dropdown — search or browse
  3. Set allocation % using sliders — must total 100%
  4. Click ▶ Run Simulation
📊

What You'll See

  • 6 summary tiles — Invested, Equity Value, Dividends, Cash Accrual, Balance, Money Market benchmark
  • Interactive charts — hover for month-by-month growth and dividend details
  • Monthly breakdown table — click any highlighted cell for per-ticker detail
  • Tax impact section — adjust tax rate to see yearly liability on dividends and interest
  • Annual returns — pre-tax performance per calendar year
💡

Key Things to Know

  • All summary values are clickable — cards, table cells, and chart points reveal detailed breakdowns
  • If a ticker has no data for a month, its allocation is redistributed to available tickers
  • The MMF column shows what the same money would earn in a money market fund — a risk-free benchmark
