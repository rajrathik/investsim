Investment Analysis Tools

A suite of tools to explore historical market data, analyze sector performance, and simulate investment strategies. All data is sourced from Yahoo Finance and FRED — no projections, no guesswork.

💼
Asset Allocation Simulator
Simulate monthly investing across any mix of assets. Uses real historical prices to model whole-share purchases and dividend cash flows.
Launch Simulator How It Works
📈
Sector Performance
Annual total returns and dividend payouts for all 11 S&P 500 sector ETFs plus VTI. Compare year-by-year with a color-coded quilt table.
🔗
Correlation Matrix
See how closely sectors move together. Low-correlation sectors can reduce portfolio volatility without sacrificing returns.
📉
Drawdown Analysis
Peak-to-trough loss analysis for each sector. Understand worst-case scenarios and how long recoveries took historically.
🔄
Sector Rotation
Year-by-year sector rankings #1 to #12 by performance. No sector leads every year — see the pattern.
💰
Dividend Growth
Year-over-year dividend growth for each sector ETF. Identify consistent payers vs sectors that cut dividends.
📊
$10K Growth Chart
How would $10,000 invested in each sector have grown? Compare cumulative growth across sectors over any time range.
⚖️
Risk vs Return
Scatter plot of each sector by annual volatility (risk) vs total return. Find efficient sectors in the upper-left sweet spot.
